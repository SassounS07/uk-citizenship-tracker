    Please read

    This is a planning aid, not legal advice. It shows estimates based on the dates you enter.

    • Time outside the UK: for settlement, usually no more than 180 days in any 12 months. For citizenship, no more than 450 days in the 5 years before applying (270 in 3 years if married to a British citizen) and no more than 90 days in the last 12 months. The Home Office can use discretion on small overages.
    • Earned Settlement was confirmed on 2 November 2026. Rules are expected from December 2026, phased in through 2027. Applications are decided under the rules in force on the decision date.
    • The English level for settlement rises from B1 to B2 for applications made from 26 March 2027.
